Why this module

Inventory NRV Write-down (IAS 2)

IAS 2.9

Lower of cost and NRV, done properly

Each line records cost and net realisable value; the required write-down is the excess of cost over NRV, floored at zero so it never carries inventory above cost. The run totals the closing write-down and shows the exact movement before you post anything.

IAS 2.33

Recoveries that cannot overshoot

When NRV recovers, the module reverses the write-down but only up to the amount previously recognised. Because every line floors its write-down at zero, a recovery can release the allowance back toward zero and no further, so the new carrying amount is the lower of cost and the revised NRV.

IAS 2.34

Posted figures that stay put

Once a run is posted it recognises a balanced, sealed ledger movement. Its date, accounts, journal and inventory lines are frozen against ORM writes, line inserts, moves and deletes. To change it you reverse it, and only an EH Accounting Manager can do that.

Day in the life

A period-end inventory review, start to ledger

At close you open a new NRV run for the reporting date, add a line per finished-goods or raw-material group, and enter each item's cost and net realisable value. The run rolls the opening write-down forward from the last posted run for the same product and flags any line whose opening does not tie out. You compute, review the movement, then post: the manager-gated entry debits the write-down expense and credits the allowance, or releases the allowance on a recovery, capped at what was recognised before. The run and its entry are stamped, sealed and frozen for the audit file.

Edge cases

The cases most modules quietly ignore.

In the shipped code today, each one a place where a cheaper module silently does the wrong thing.

Bounded measurement

Cost, NRV, opening allowance and required allowance are non-negative and cost-bounded, so neither bad input nor a recovery can carry inventory outside its economic limits.

Gross and net movement

The run stores gross write-downs and gross reversals for IAS 2.36, then posts their net ledger movement so repeated closes do not double-charge profit or loss.

Controlled opening

Automatic openings refresh from the latest posted product close in one batched lookup. Any manual override is explicit, manager-stamped and blocked from posting until it carries a reason.

Evidence stays linked

Movement entries, reversal entries and superseded revisions each have direct navigation and search filters, preserving the full correction chain for review.

Zero-movement close

A legitimate nil movement posts a sealed subledger closing anchor without manufacturing an empty journal entry, so the next period still receives an audited opening.

What is inside

Built to do the job, end to end.

  • eh.nrv.run. The period-end assessment model with immutable per-date revisions, item or category basis, totals for cost, closing allowance, gross write-downs, gross reversals and net movement, plus compute and posting stamps.
  • eh.nrv.line. One bounded inventory measurement with non-negative cost and NRV, an optional product link, a batched prior-close roll-forward, and an explicit reasoned manager override when external opening evidence is required.
  • Movement posting engine. Posting validates distinct typed accounts and a general journal, builds a balanced sealed entry, and records a zero-movement closing anchor when no ledger delta is needed. Reversal uses the manager-selected open accounting date and retains a direct evidence link.
  • Security and isolation. Preparers can navigate, create, edit and delete drafts; managers control posting, reversal and opening overrides; auditors remain read-only. Global record rules scope both models to allowed companies.
  • Views and audit trail. List, form, search, graph and pivot views expose movement, reversal and prior-revision evidence, gross and net disclosure measures, opening provenance, status and chatter under the Inventory NRV menu.

Honest about the edges

What this does not do, so nothing surprises you.

  • It does not read stock valuation or quantities from Inventory automatically; you enter cost and net realisable value per line, optionally linking a product for the roll-forward.
  • It does not calculate net realisable value for you; the estimated selling price less costs to complete and sell is your input.
  • It posts the write-down and its reversal only; it does not adjust inventory valuation layers or the standard or average cost on the product.
  • You choose item-by-item or grouped-category assessment. Category mode nets only lines in the same product category and allocates the bounded requirement over deficit lines; it never nets unrelated categories.
  • It provides gross write-down, gross reversal and net movement measures plus the audit trail, but does not format a complete financial-statement disclosure note.
  • It does not import prior write-downs from outside this module; the automatic roll-forward reads prior posted runs here, while an external opening requires a reasoned manager override.
