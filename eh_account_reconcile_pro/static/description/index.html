Day in the life

A bookkeeper clears a month of bank lines.

A bank journal opens a searchable, date- and direction-filtered workspace with paged unreconciled lines. For each line the engine scores candidate journal items by company-currency amount, date, partner, reference overlap, and 90-day history, and shows the confidence breakdown. Most lines have a clear top candidate, which the bookkeeper confirms with one click or by dragging the candidate onto the line. Several candidates can be matched to one deposit in a single action. A proven zero-foreign, non-zero-company FX remainder can be routed to the configured exchange gain or loss account by residual sign under a hard small-variance cap. Every accepted, manual, bulk, skip, or write-off decision is written to append-only audit evidence. At close, the exception PDF separates gross exposure from net movement and keeps mixed-currency totals in currency buckets.

Edge cases

The cases most modules quietly ignore.

In the shipped code today, each one a place where a cheaper module silently does the wrong thing.

SEALED ADJUSTMENTS

The original posted statement move remains untouched. A new balanced, sealed adjustment carries company and transaction currency on both legs; locked dates fail before a move is created.

VERIFIED PROVENANCE

Suggestion provenance is recomputed on the server, session counters update atomically, and a narrow advisory lock serialises only duplicate opens for the same user and journal.

FX RESIDUAL

Only a proven zero-foreign, non-zero-company remainder can use FX gain or loss accounts, under a hard server-owned cap. Unconfigured accounts or ordinary bank variance are refused.

COMPANY SAFETY

Rules, journals, accounts, partners, candidates, and history are constrained to the active company. Invalid regexes, amount bands, and cross-company targets fail closed.

What is inside

Built to do the job, end to end.

  • Two-pane reconciliation workspace. Search by reference or partner, filter by direction and date, and page through the complete open-line population. Candidate search also covers registered-payment outstanding accounts.
  • Five-signal suggestion engine. Amounts are normalised to company currency; rule regex evaluation and history retrieval are batched once per statement line.
  • User-defined reconciliation rules. Company-safe journal, account, and partner scopes combine with regex, amount band, direction, write-off amount type, and confidence boost.
  • Append-only audit log. Match and write-off evidence is created only by sealed server workflows; ordinary callers cannot edit or delete it.
  • FX residual write-off. A proven FX-only residual uses the configured gain or loss account under a hard cap and a database savepoint.
  • Reconciliation exception PDF. Gross exposure, net movement, inflows, and outflows are reported per journal; totals remain separated by currency.

Honest about the edges

What this does not do, so nothing surprises you.

  • Safe auto-reconciliation is available, but it requires a high, unambiguous score and an exact company-currency amount unless a specific auto-confirm rule proves the decision.
  • Statement filters are session-local; saved filter presets and amount-range filtering are not included.
  • Matching is 1-to-N. Matching several statement lines to one journal item in a single action is not supported.
  • Match and write-off posting run inside database savepoints, and the original posted statement move remains immutable.
  • Rules add score boosts and audit codes; they do not replace Odoo reconciliation models.
  • Audit rows capture actor, time, source, confidence, rule codes, decision group, and sealed adjusting move; free-text override reasons are not included.
