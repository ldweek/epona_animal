Day in the life

A customer settles up before month-end without emailing anyone.

A buyer logs into the portal and lands on the home page. The AR card already shows three open invoices, the total overdue, and that the oldest is 41 days past due. They pick a date range, click once, and download a statement PDF that matches the seller's books to the cent. Need last quarter's position for an audit? They set the end date back and the statement rewinds the reconciliations booked since, showing exactly what was open then. The accounts team never touched it.

Edge cases

The cases most modules quietly ignore.

In the shipped code today, each one a place where a cheaper module silently does the wrong thing.

BACK-DATED ACCURACY

Ask for a statement as at a past date and it reverses the partial reconciliations created after that date, so historical open balances match what was actually outstanding then, not today's residual. Most portal add-ons just print today's numbers under yesterday's heading.

BRANDED REFUSALS

Invalid dates, excessive ranges, a busy render lock, a missing statement engine, and render failures each produce a clear branded page with an appropriate status instead of a stack trace.

DURABLE AUDIT

Every successful render records its execution, normalized options, and exact PDF hash. The source PDF payload is deliberately not retained as a reusable attachment.

COMPANY CURRENCY

The card totals only positive overdue customer invoices in the active company and displays the active company's currency. Credits, refunds, unapplied payments, and sister-company entries never net the figure.

PRIVATE DELIVERY

The authenticated response is marked no-store and carries safe ASCII and UTF-8 filenames. Partner and company scope always come from the signed-in session.

CHEAP AT SCALE

The overdue total and oldest due date come from a single grouped SQL read, so the home card stays fast even for a partner carrying many open lines.

What is inside

Built to do the job, end to end.

  • Statement selection form. The portal card offers required start and end dates plus open-item or activity type. The authenticated route validates the range against an administrator-configured maximum and serves the rendered PDF inline.
  • Portal home AR card. Shows open customer-invoice count, positive overdue total, oldest overdue invoice date, and days overdue in the active company's currency, all aggregated server side.
  • Audited statement generator. Reuses the existing customer-statement handler and records execution options plus the exact output hash without retaining a replayable customer PDF payload.
  • Administrator and website controls. A settings field governs the maximum statement period, while Odoo's website editor can show or hide the customizable card.

Honest about the edges

What this does not do, so nothing surprises you.

  • Each successful render has a durable execution row, normalized options, and exact PDF hash, but the customer PDF bytes are intentionally not retained for later replay.
  • Portal queries are explicitly scoped to the signed-in commercial partner and active company. The feature does not combine sister-company ledgers into a consolidated statement.
  • There is no credit-limit integration. The AR card shows open customer invoices and overdue invoices only; it does not surface a credit limit or headroom.
  • It adds statement download and AR summary capabilities. It does not add online payment, dispute, or messaging flows of its own.
  • Requires eh_account_dynamic_reports for the statement engine and an authenticated portal user. It does not generate statements for anonymous visitors.
